Recommendation 2. We recommend against using qSOFA compared to SIRS, NEWS, or MEWS as a single screening tool for sepsis or septic shock Strong recommendation, moderate-quality evidence Rationale The qSOFA uses 3 variables to predict death and prolonged ICU stay in patients with known or suspected sepsis: a Glasgow Coma Score < 15, a respiratory rate ≥ 22 breaths/min and a systolic blood pressure ≤ 100 mmHg. When any two of these variables are present simultaneously the patient is considered to be qSOFA positive.
